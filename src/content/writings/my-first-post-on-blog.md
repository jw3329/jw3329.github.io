Since starting my personal blog, this is my first post. I've decided to write about my daily feelings here — not just about development, but also about things I experience in my personal life.

I used to write in a diary, and I thought it was meaningful to write things down on paper. However, I rarely had the time to write quickly, and it took extra effort just to open the diary. So instead, this will be my first post.

I don't know how long this will continue, but I will do my best to keep writing here.

I will record what I felt during work, as well as my personal learnings, projects, and startups I’m currently working on. Hopefully, these posts will be valuable in the future.
